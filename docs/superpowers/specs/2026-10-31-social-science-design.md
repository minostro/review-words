# Social Science — October 2026

User-approved design: one child deck named `Salvador::Social Science - Oct - 2026`.
Purpose is comprehension of the nine supplied worksheets, not spelling. Categories
are school places, school workers, family relationships, settlements, community
helpers, and community organisation. Categories are tags/labels, never subdecks.

Each card contains one short written English question, an original worksheet
illustration/photo (without printed/handwritten answer labels), three plausible
answer choices, optional manual question audio, and an answer-side explanation.
Use varied questions (functions, relationships, comparisons, situations), deduplicate
repeated worksheet material, and do not multiply near-identical variations.
Tapping an answer locks the attempt, gives feedback, and leaves Anki's native
Show Answer/rating flow manual. Wrong attempts or revealing an unknown answer should be rated Again. Hearing
the question alone is an accessibility aid and does not require Again.
Do not automatically speak either side. Questions, not answers, are narrated.

Pictures provide meaningful context but must not reveal the correct text or make
unrelated comprehension trivial. A picture-choice question can use three worksheet
pictures; a text-choice question can show a relevant scene or all candidate images.
Family picture has handwritten labels: use carefully bounded image regions or a
neutral family crop and never include those labels on question sides.
