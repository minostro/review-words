# English spelling artwork

Created using the built-in image-generation tool, from the user's school-sheet
references. These are single sprite atlas assets displayed through CSS windows;
the bitmap outputs were copied unchanged. No external media is loaded in Anki.

- `images/unit1.png`: four by four grid, row-major positions defined in `english_spelling_content.py`.
- `images/unit2.png`: three by three grid; calendar reused for weekdays, with a seven-dot position indicator and spoken day name.

## Unit 1 generation prompt

Create ONE educational illustration sprite atlas as a single square image, 2048x2048. Use the attached school vocabulary sheet as subject/style reference only. Clean, cheerful school worksheet clipart, softly shaded colored flat illustrations with clear dark contours, solid pure white background. NO words, NO letters, NO numbers, NO logos, NO scribbles, NO grid lines. Exact rigid 4 columns x 4 rows, 16 equal square cells of 512px. Center each illustration in its cell, within middle 75%, leaving ample white gutters so each cell can be displayed separately in a flashcard using CSS. Keep illustrations separate, never crossing cell boundaries. Exact row-major contents: ROW1: 1 cultivated green FIELD with gently striped crops and far hills; 2 dense FOREST with several trees; 3 clump of fresh green GRASS with visible blades; 4 blue oval LAKE enclosed on all sides by grassy land. ROW2: 1 rocky peaked MOUNTAIN; 2 winding blue RIVER extending through green banks; 3 exactly ONE green LEAF; 4 exactly THREE separate green LEAVES. ROW3: 1 single grey ROCK; 2 fully clothed school-age child seated at breakfast table with bowl cereal, toast and orange juice; 3 close-up hands being washed with soap under running tap over sink; 4 fully clothed child drinking a glass of water. ROW4: 1 child having a shower, modestly shown shoulders/head only with rest of body fully obscured by opaque shower curtain, water overhead; 2 folded bath TOWEL with simple stripes; 3 one TOOTHBRUSH with bristles clearly visible; 4 one TOOTHPASTE tube with cap and small paste swirl, completely blank tube without any text. Prioritize clear visual distinctions and correct exact tile order. This is one sprite atlas image for a children's Anki deck, not a labeled worksheet.

## Unit 2 generation prompt

Create ONE square educational illustration sprite atlas, exactly 3 columns x 3 rows of nine equal square cells. Image 1 is subject reference from a school sheet; Image 2 is STYLE reference: match its cheerful clean colored school clipart, softly shaded cartoon, dark contours, pure white backdrop. No text, letters, numbers, logos, grid lines or labels anywhere. Center every illustration in middle 75% of its cell, generous white gutters, objects never cross boundaries. Exact 9 cells in row-major order. ROW1: (1) colorful desk calendar with completely blank white page, no numbers or letters, teal header and two binder rings; (2) fully clothed school-age child wearing headphones listening to music, small musical notes floating; (3) child at a laptop typing an email, screen with a clear envelope symbol and simple neutral lines but no letters or words. ROW2: (1) child roller skating with helmet and knee pads; (2) smiling child reading an open colorful comic book with tiny picture panels, no text; (3) person going shopping, carrying colorful shopping bags. ROW3: (1) child seated on a sofa watching a television showing a friendly cartoon animal; (2) school-age child helping an elderly person with a walking stick to cross a zebra crossing, friendly cooperative scene for 'must'; (3) a yellow pencil snapped in two inside a clear red prohibition circle and diagonal slash for 'must not break pencils'. This is a single 3x3 sprite atlas image used via CSS cell windows in offline flashcards. Keep exactly this layout, square cells, no written English answers.
